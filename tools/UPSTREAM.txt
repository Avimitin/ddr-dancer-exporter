have separate rights and are not covered by the parser's GPL license.

Local DDR extension is implemented in scripts/preview_motion.py:
curve 0x001e is decoded as half-float XYZ translation. Upstream files are
kept unchanged. Camera decoding is local and is labelled as inferred.
