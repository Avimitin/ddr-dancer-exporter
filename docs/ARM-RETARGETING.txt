Arm correction applied to converted exports
==========================================
Affected: Ano, Nailong, Adelie Tomorin. Original Miku and stock Emi are unchanged.

The exporter now aligns each arm chain from its actual source rest frame to
DDR's collar, shoulder, elbow and wrist anchors. Bone-axis length is adjusted
without uniformly shrinking the arm/wing cross-section. Vertex corrections
are blended using the skin weights; normals use the inverse-transpose of the
same correction. All output still uses the 33-bone DDR rig and nine packets.

Ano's imported arm/wrist twist and auxiliary forearm groups now map to DDR
ArmRoll/ForeArmRoll. Nailong and Tomorin get a gradual spatial split between
main and roll bones. KTMDL's four-influence limit is enforced by retaining the
four largest weights and renormalizing. arm-retarget.json records the maximum
discarded weight and actual roll weight totals per model.

Validation: binary decode and DDS validation for all three models; 65 HT01
poses per model before/after; arm joint endpoint/direction checks; handedness;
finite positions and normals. validation.json records quantitative comparisons.
ht01-before-after.jpg shows actual old versus new exported KTMDL geometry at
0 and 16 seconds. The videos in the main viewer are rebuilt from new exports.

Residual limitations: the torso/legs keep their original proportional fitting.
Broad bodies and wings can still overlap in lowered-arm poses. There is no
collision solver, cloth/wing physics, or finger animation. This fixes the
incorrect arm bind frame and missing roll-weight distribution; it does not
provide perfect full-body retargeting for arbitrary nonhuman anatomy.
Actual DDR loading/rendering remains untested.

Previous outputs, intermediates and MP4s are preserved in:
  backups/before-arm-fix/dancers/<model>/

Rebuild:
  Blender inputs: import_blend.py, then compare_ddr.py (with --dancer)
  export_ddr.py --dancer <id>
  validate_export.py --dancer <id>
  build_previews.py --dancer <id> --all
  scripts/diagnostics/check_arm_fix.py compares to the preserved before-arm-fix baseline.
